About

I'm an AI Engineer based in Jaipur, working at the point where LLM research meets production infrastructure. Most of my day is spent on agent orchestration, retrieval quality, and the unglamorous parts — latency budgets, token cost, multi-tenant isolation, and queues that don't fall over.

At Neerja Softwares I scaled a multi-agent chatbot platform from prototype to a production SaaS serving several deployment surfaces, with tool-calling, MCP integration and RAG pipelines behind it. Before that I led AI development for a fitness app at Quest Digiflex — food calorie estimation, pose classification, and an LLM assistant — and configured a Phi-3.5 deployment for deterministic, reproducible inference on a contract basis.

I also like the coordination side of the work: I ran an event website and RSVP platform end to end, from client communication to WhatsApp automation for registrations and reminders. Outside of work you'll find me implementing papers (federated learning, Thompson sampling) and contributing to open source — I picked up a Pull Shark badge during Hacktoberfest.
